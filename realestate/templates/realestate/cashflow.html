PACT Voucher Glossary

BRV (Bank Receipt Voucher):
Bulk deposit of cash or cheques grouped into the bank.
CRO / CRV (Cash Receipt Voucher):
Cash physically collected directly from a tenant.
CRT (Converted Receipt Voucher):
Direct electronic transfer or standalone cheque straight to the bank.
CBR (Cheque Bounce Receipt):
Reversal record created when a cheque fails to clear.
PDR (Post Dated Receipt):
A PDC physically held by the company but not yet banked.
CRVM / IRVM (Manual Receipt):
Manually adjusted or internal system receipt records.
